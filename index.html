The horizontal bracket compares Krt with the asymptote Kr(t − τ) at the same output. The vertical bracket measures the actual error e(t) = Krt − y(t).

e(t) = Krτ(1 − e−t/τ) approaches ess = Krτ as t → ∞. The finite-time error is not exactly its limiting value; rounded numbers may look equal.

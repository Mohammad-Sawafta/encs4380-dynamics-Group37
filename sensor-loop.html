Equations, solver, and what to look for

The sensor state obeys y_s' = (y - y_s) / tau_s. At every RK4 stage, the PID receives y_s, calculates e = r - y_s, and its clipped command drives the original plant. Plant, sensor, integral, and derivative-filter states are integrated together. The solver step bound includes the sensor rate 1 / tau_s.

At tau_s = 0, the sensor is bypassed algebraically instead of dividing by zero. That case calls the P1 simulation directly and reproduces its output, measurement, and control arrays exactly.

For the first-order example with Kp = 2 and Ki = 8, increasing tau_s adds phase lag. The sensor says the output is still low after the plant has crossed the target, so the PID continues to push. Overshoot grows; near tau_s = 0.6 s the oscillations stop decaying, and beyond it they grow.

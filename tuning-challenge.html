Challenge rules and tuning hints

Each new round draws K between 0.7 and 1.5 and tau between 0.6 s and 2.0 s for tau y' + y = K u. The same hidden plant remains active across retries. A new plant resets the attempt and best-score counters.

Kp usually shortens rise time but cannot remove all step error. Ki removes step error but too much can add overshoot. Kd reacts to motion and can add damping. A metric not reached within the 18-second simulation fails its requirement.
